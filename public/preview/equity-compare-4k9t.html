Equity & credit

Assumptions.
  • Illustration only. Not a loan offer, rate quote or approval. Rates, fees and limits are entered by the loan officer and must come from current pricing.
  • Card payments are held at the amount entered; real minimums usually fall as balances drop. Installment debts amortize over the months left.
  • Paying off debt with home equity turns unsecured debt into debt secured by the home. Paid-off cards are assumed to stay open with a zero balance.
  • Figure HELOC is modeled as a fixed-rate, fully amortizing loan with the full line drawn at closing and the origination fee deducted from the draw. Redraws are not modeled.
  • Interest deductibility is not considered. Borrowers should consult a tax professional.
