# Military pay dates 2027 — official sources (verified by Claude Chat, Nov 13, 2026)

## Active-duty payday rule
DoD FMR Vol. 7A, ch. 1 (https://comptroller.war.gov/Portals/45/documents/fmr/current/07a/07a_01.pdf):
- 010701: "The payday is the first calendar day of the month after the month in which the entitlement was earned."
- 010702: a payday on a Saturday, Sunday or federal holiday may be paid on the earlier workday, "but not more than 3 days before the scheduled payday."
- Mid-month payday on the 15th: DFAS practice (paydays on the 1st and 15th); FMR section 0107 cites 37 U.S.C. 1014.

## VA compensation payment date rule
38 U.S.C. 5120(e) (https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/5120): "Whenever the first day of any calendar month falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal public holiday (as defined in section 6103 of title 5), the Secretary shall, to the maximum extent practicable, certify benefit payments for such month in such a way that such payments will be delivered ... on the Friday immediately preceding such Saturday or Sunday, or in the case of a legal holiday, the weekday (other than Saturday) immediately preceding such legal public holiday ..."
VA pays each month's benefit on the 1st of the following month (in arrears). VA does not publish an annual payment calendar.
Golden list (benefit month → paid): Dec 2026 → Thu Dec 31, 2026 · Jan 2027 → Mon Feb 1 · Feb → Mon Mar 1 · Mar → Thu Apr 1 · Apr → Fri Apr 30 · May → Tue Jun 1 · Jun → Thu Jul 1 · Jul → Fri Jul 30 · Aug → Wed Sep 1 · Sep → Fri Oct 1 · Oct → Mon Nov 1 · Nov → Wed Dec 1 · Dec 2027 → Fri Dec 31, 2027.
