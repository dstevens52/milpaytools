These are the **January 2026** tables. They predate DTMO's TX270 (Abilene/Dyess AFB) temporary
increase effective May 16, 2026, which was hand-applied to `src/data/bah/2026/mhaRates.ts`
(source record: `bah-2026-abilene-temporary-increase.md`). DTMO's current 2026 zip carries the
increased TX270 rows as `bahw26.txt` / `bahwo26.txt` and the January rows as "old" files.

Re-importing these files (`npm run bah:import -- --year 2026 --src data/sources/dtmo/2026 --prior 2025 --out <scratch dir>`)
reproduces `zipToMha.ts` and `mhaNames.ts` byte-for-byte and every `mhaRates.ts` row except
TX270. `tests/e2e/bah-release-day.spec.ts` checks this.
