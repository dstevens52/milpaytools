## Definitions (NR26)
- Group A: the sponsor's initial enlistment or appointment began before Jan. 1, 2018.
- Group B: the sponsor's initial enlistment or appointment began on or after Jan. 1, 2018.
- Premium-based plans (TRICARE Reserve Select, TRICARE Retired Reserve, TRICARE Young Adult) follow TRICARE Select Group B deductibles, copays and caps; only the premium is set separately (NR26, TM27).
- Active duty service members have no out-of-pocket costs (TM27).

## Open Season (OS27)
- TRICARE Open Season: Nov. 9–Dec. 8, 2026. Changes take effect Jan. 1, 2027.
- To keep your current plan, no action is needed; coverage continues in 2027 while eligible.
- Federal Benefits Open Season (dental/vision, FEDVIP): Nov. 9–Dec. 14, 2026.
- Outside Open Season, Prime/Select enrollment changes need a TRICARE Qualifying Life Event.

"Unless otherwise noted, costs are for 2027 and reflect family coverage for two adults and two children." Footnotes (OS27, paraphrased): FEHB = a sample FEHB family plan. Marketplace = two nonsmoking adults and two children in ZIP 80202 (Denver, CO), without premium tax credits or other subsidies; actual costs vary by location, age, household, income and plan. Marketplace plans: enrollees pay 100% of nonemergency non-network care. Employer = nationwide averages, employee contribution only; deductible is the average family PPO deductible (aggregate); no comparable family out-of-pocket max was available. Source for employer figures: Kaiser Family Foundation 2026 Employer-Sponsored Health Benefits Survey. TRICARE calls its annual out-of-pocket maximum the catastrophic cap.
