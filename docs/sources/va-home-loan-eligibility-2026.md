# VA home loan eligibility: service requirements for a COE

Verified by Claude Chat on 2026-11-21.
Source: https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/eligibility/ ("Last updated: September 22, 2026"). Quoted from the page; paraphrase only where marked.

- **Current active-duty service members:** "at least 90 continuous days".
- **Veterans, Gulf War period** (VA lists this as "Between August 2, 1990, and the present"). Any one of:
  - "At least 24 continuous months"
  - "The full period (at least 90 days) for which you were called or ordered to active duty"
  - (paraphrase) at least 90 days and discharged under a qualifying exception
  - (paraphrase) less than 90 days and discharged for a service-connected disability
- **Veterans of other periods:** (paraphrase) the thresholds differ by era. Most are 90 or 181 days or 24 continuous months. Link to VA's page rather than listing every era.
- **National Guard:** any one of:
  - "at least 90 days of non-training active-duty Title 10 service"
  - (paraphrase) at least 90 days of active duty, including at least 30 consecutive days, with a DD214 showing the required 32 USC activation
  - (paraphrase) 6 creditable years and still serving
  - (paraphrase) 6 creditable years and discharged honorably or placed on the retired list
- **Selected Reserve:** any one of:
  - (paraphrase) at least 90 days of non-training active-duty service
  - "6 creditable years in the Selected Reserve, and you continue to serve"
  - (paraphrase) 6 creditable years and discharged honorably or placed on the retired list
