# Education benefit rates 2026–27 (official source record)

**Retrieved and verified:** 2026-11-12 by Claude Chat, from the live VA pages below (rendered page text).

## Post-9/11 GI Bill (Chapter 33) — effective Aug 1, 2026 to Jul 31, 2027
Source: https://www.va.gov/education/benefit-rates/post-9-11-gi-bill-rates/ (page last updated Oct 13, 2026)

**Eligibility tiers (percentage of full benefit):**
| Active-duty service after Sept 10, 2001 | Tier |
|---|---|
| At least 36 months (or 30+ days with service-connected discharge, or Purple Heart) | 100% |
| 910 to 1,094 days (30 to 35 months) | 90% |
| 730 to 909 days (24 to 29 months) | 80% |
| 545 to 729 days (18 to 23 months) | 70% |
| 180 to 544 days (6 to 17 months) | 60% |
| 90 to 179 days (90 days to 5 months) | 50% |
There is **no 40% tier.** Statute: 38 U.S.C. § 3313(c) — reduced rates of 90, 80, 70, 60 and 50 percent; each applies to tuition, the monthly housing allowance and the books stipend.

**Housing allowance (MHA) rules (VA.gov text):**
- "To be eligible for MHA, your rate of pursuit must be more than 50%."
- Rate of pursuit example: 9 of 12 credits = 80% ("9 divided by 12, rounded to the nearest tenth").
- MHA is prorated by **both** the eligibility tier and the rate of pursuit.
- Location-based MHA = E-5 with-dependents BAH at the ZIP where the student physically attends most classes.
- **Online-only:** "We base your monthly housing allowance on half the national average for MHA. We'll pay you up to **$1,198**." (2025–26 was $1,169 per the 2025–2026 past-rates page.) Note: taking at least 1 class in person changes this.

**Tuition and books:**
- Private or foreign school: net tuition and mandatory fees up to **$30,908.34** per academic year.
- Public school: in-state tuition and fees paid in full (at the tier percentage).
- Books and supplies: up to **$1,000** per academic year (prorated by tier).

## Montgomery GI Bill Active Duty (Chapter 30) — effective Oct 1, 2026 to Sep 30, 2027
Source: https://www.va.gov/education/benefit-rates/montgomery-active-duty-rates/
Institutional training, served 3+ years (or 2 years when the enlistment was under 3 years):
- Full time: **$2,601.00** per full month
- 75%: **$1,950.75**
- 50%: **$1,300.50**
(2025–26 full-time rate in the repo: $2,518.)

## VR&E (Chapter 31) subsistence allowance — FY2027, effective Oct 1, 2026
Source: https://www.benefits.va.gov/vocrehab/vrerates27.asp
Standard rates when the Veteran is **not** eligible for the Post-9/11 GI Bill housing-rate option. Institutional training:

| Training time | No dependents | One dependent | Two dependents | Each additional dependent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full time | $837.23 | $1,038.49 | $1,223.79 | $89.18 |
| ¾ time | $629.08 | $780.00 | $914.97 | $68.60 |
| ½ time | $420.92 | $521.51 | $613.01 | $45.75 |

- Maximum monthly Chapter 31 subsistence (FY2027): $3,542.47.
- Rule (https://www.benefits.va.gov/vocrehab/subsistence_allowance_rates.asp): a Veteran who qualifies for the Post-9/11 GI Bill may elect the Post-9/11 BAH-based rate for subsistence. Otherwise the standard rates above apply. Chapter 31 pays subsistence at half time (unlike Chapter 33 MHA).

## MGIB-AD (Chapter 30) eligibility — service length
Source: https://www.va.gov/education/about-gi-bill-benefits/montgomery-active-duty/ (retrieved 2026-11-12 by Claude Chat, "Am I eligible" section)
Category I (the category that applies to post-1985 entrants) requires an honorable discharge, a high school diploma/GED or 12 college credits, a $100/month pay reduction for the first 12 months, **and continuous active-duty service of at least 3 years, or 2 years if that was the enlistment agreement, or 4 years when the member entered the Selected Reserve within a year of leaving active duty ("2 by 4").** Other categories cover pre-1985 entrants and special conversions. Exceptions for some early separations exist; the calculator should not model them, only note that they exist.
