## Notes
- A third-party repost of the DoD 2026 announcement listed Staten Island and Seattle at 5%. The official DTMO PDF above shows **4%** for both. The PDF governs.
- Monterey, the National Capital Region, Orange County, Ventura, Connecticut, Northern New Jersey, Newport RI and Boston are **not** 2026 CONUS COLA areas.
- CONUS COLA is taxable. The monthly amount depends on pay grade, years of service and dependents; the official amount is available from DTMO's CONUS COLA Rate Lookup: https://www.travel.dod.mil/Allowances/CONUS-Cost-of-Living-Allowance/CONUS-COLA-Rate-Lookup/
- Policy source: DoD FMR Vol. 7A, Ch. 67 — https://comptroller.defense.gov/Portals/45/documents/fmr/current/07a/07a_67.pdf
- Eligibility threshold: non-housing cost of living at least 8% above the national average (107% threshold), per the DTMO CONUS COLA page.
