# VA IRRRL and COE: what VA says

Verified by Claude Chat on 2026-11-21.

## IRRRL (VA.gov)
Source: https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-types/interest-rate-reduction-loan/ ("Last updated: January 7, 2026").
- What it can do:
  - It may help you lower "your monthly mortgage payment by getting you a lower interest rate".
  - It may help you move from an adjustable-rate loan to a fixed one, which makes payments "more stable".
- Eligibility:
  - "You're using the IRRRL to refinance your existing VA-backed home loan."
  - "You can certify that you currently live in or used to live in the home covered by the loan."
- **VA.gov's IRRRL page says nothing about appraisals or credit underwriting.**

## IRRRL (VA Lenders Handbook, M26-7, Chapter 6, Topic 1)
Source: the Chapter 6 text dated 11-01-24, read from a lender-hosted copy (https://www.tenaco.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/VA-Pamphlet-26-7-Chapter-6-Refinancing-Loans-11-01-24.pdf). The same sentence appears in VA's own archived Chapter 6.
- "Generally, no appraisal, credit information or underwriting is required on an IRRRL, and any lender may close an IRRRL automatically."
- Older edition, same chapter: "Although VA does not require an appraisal or credit underwriting on IRRRLs, any customary and reasonable credit report or appraisal expense incurred by a lender to satisfy its lending requirements may be charged to the borrower".

Wording to use on MilPayTools: "VA generally doesn't require an appraisal or credit underwriting for an IRRRL. Lenders can still require them under their own rules."

## ARM to fixed
- Use VA.gov's wording: an IRRRL can move you from an adjustable rate to a fixed rate, which can make payments more stable.
- Don't say VA "considers" this a net tangible benefit. That isn't verified here.
- The 200-basis-point rule in `va-refinance-rules-2026.md` covers fixed rate → adjustable rate, not the other direction.

## Certificate of Eligibility (COE)
Source: https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/how-to-request-coe/ ("Last updated: September 22, 2026").
- Three ways to request a COE:
  - "Option 1: Online"
  - "Option 2: Through your lender" (the lender may use VA's Web LGY system)
  - "Option 3: By mail" (VA Form 26-1880, sent to the regional loan center address on the form)
- **The page doesn't mention eBenefits.** Replace every eBenefits COE link with this VA.gov page.
- Online request page: https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/request-coe-form-26-1880 ("Sign in to request a COE, get your COE, or check your status").
