# Rank page titles and descriptions — click-rate batch 1 (Nov 2026)

## Change
`/pay/[rank]` titles switched from the Army-only rank name ("E-3 (Private First Class) Pay 2026 | MilPayTools")
to a branch-neutral absolute title with the 2026 basic-pay range ("E-3 Pay 2026: $2,837–$3,198/Month, All Branches").
Meta descriptions now lead with every branch's rank title (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine, and Space Force where it fits),
then the pay range, then what's on the page. H1, body copy, URLs, structured data and figures are unchanged.

**Why:** people search by branch ("e3 pay air force", "how much does an e3 make in the navy"), and the Army name
misleads for some grades (Staff Sergeant is E-6 in the Army but E-5 in the Air Force).
