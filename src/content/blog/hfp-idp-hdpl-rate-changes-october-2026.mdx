Effective October 1, 2026, three deployment-related pays changed. Hostile Fire Pay (HFP) rose to $450/month. Imminent Danger Pay (IDP) rose to $275/month, prorated at $9.16/day. And the top Hardship Duty Pay–Location (HDP-L) rate went *down*, from $150 to $100/month. Most coverage stopped at the first two numbers. The HDP-L cut is the part that decides whether a given paycheck actually gets bigger.

## Combat pay went up. Hardship duty pay went down.

Before October 1, HFP and IDP had both been a flat $225/month since October 2002, according to the DoD release announcing the change. The new rates come from a memo signed July 14, 2026 by the Assistant Secretary of War for Manpower and Reserve Affairs. The same memo cut the top HDP-L rate.

## What changed

| Pay | Before Oct 1, 2026 | From Oct 1, 2026 | How it's paid |
|-----|-------------------|------------------|---------------|
| Hostile Fire Pay (HFP) | $225/month | $450/month | Full monthly amount, not prorated |
| Imminent Danger Pay (IDP) | $225/month | $275/month | Prorated at $9.16/day, up to $275/month |
| Hardship Duty Pay–Location (HDP-L), top rate | $150/month | $100/month | Monthly, by location designation |
| HDP-L, other rates | $50 and $100/month | $50 and $100/month | Unchanged |

Pay for periods before October 1, 2026 was figured at the old rates. A September deployment month doesn't get the new amounts after the fact.

One structural difference is worth knowing. HFP is paid as a full month: if you qualify, you get the full $450. IDP is paid by the day: $9.16 for each day in a designated imminent danger area, up to $275 for the month. For the full picture of how these fit with the other deployment pays, see [Deployment Pay Explained](/blog/deployment-pay-explained).

## What this actually means for your paycheck

The answer depends on which pays you draw. The reason is a rule that applied *before* October 1: anyone drawing HFP or IDP at a location designated at the top HDP-L rate had that HDP-L limited to $100/month, not $150. The memo ties the HDP-L reduction to the maximum allowed for combined payment of HDP-L with HFP, and of HDP-L with IDP.

So for a lot of people, the HDP-L cut doesn't change anything. They were already getting $100. Here are the cases one at a time, all for a full month.

### Drawing HFP only

- Before: $225/month
- After: $450/month
- Change: **+$225/month** ($225 × 12 = $2,700 over a full year)

The biggest winners. HFP doubled.

### Drawing IDP only

- Before: $225/month
- After: $275/month
- Change: **+$50/month** for a full month ($50 × 12 = $600 over a full year)

A partial month is paid at $9.16/day (see below), so the gain in a partial month is smaller.

### Drawing HFP or IDP *and* HDP-L at the top-rate location

Before October 1, HDP-L at the top-rate location was limited to $100/month for anyone also drawing HFP or IDP. From October 1, the top HDP-L rate is $100/month. **Their HDP-L is the same $100 either way.** All of the change comes from HFP or IDP.

| | Before | After | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| HFP + top-tier HDP-L | $225 + $100 = $325 | $450 + $100 = $550 | **+$225/month** |
| IDP + top-tier HDP-L | $225 + $100 = $325 | $275 + $100 = $375 | **+$50/month** (full month) |

For this group, the HDP-L "cut" is a wash. They come out ahead by the full HFP or IDP increase.

### Drawing HDP-L at the top rate, with no HFP or IDP

- Before: $150/month
- After: $100/month
- Change: **−$50/month** ($50 × 12 = $600 less over a full year)

This is the one group that is plainly worse off. With no HFP or IDP, the $100 limit never applied to them. They were getting the full $150, and now they get $100.

One honest caveat: the memo gives the combined-payment maximum as the reason for the change, but the reduction it states, "from $150 to $100 per month", isn't limited to members who also draw HFP or IDP. That's how we've read it here. If you're at a top-tier HDP-L location without HFP or IDP, your finance office is the place to confirm how it applies to you.

### Drawing HDP-L at the $50 or $100 rate, with no HFP or IDP

No change. Those rates didn't move.

## The IDP proration detail

IDP is paid at $9.16 for each day in a designated imminent danger area, up to $275 for the month.

| Days in an IDP area | At $9.16/day | Paid |
|---|---|---|
| 10 days | 10 × $9.16 = $91.60 | $91.60 |
| 15 days | 15 × $9.16 = $137.40 | $137.40 |
| 20 days | 20 × $9.16 = $183.20 | $183.20 |
| 31 days | 31 × $9.16 = $283.96 | $275.00 (monthly maximum) |

A full 31-day month at the daily rate would come to $283.96, which is more than the $275 monthly maximum, so the maximum governs. That's why the IDP gain for someone arriving or leaving mid-month is smaller than the full-month $50. HFP has no equivalent: it's the full $450 for a qualifying month.

## What to check on your LES

- **Look for the HFP or IDP entitlement line** (and HDP if you're at a hardship location). After October 1, 2026, HFP should show $450 for a qualifying month, IDP up to $275, and top-tier HDP-L $100.
- **Expect only one of HFP or IDP for a given period.** The memo directs the services and DFAS to make sure nobody receives both for the same period of service. If both appear for the same period, ask your finance office now, before it turns into a debt later.
- **Remember the cutover.** Days before October 1, 2026 are paid at the old rates, so a month that spans the change, or a late-posted correction for September, can show the old figures legitimately.
- **Check partial IDP months against the daily rate.** Days in the area × $9.16, up to $275.
- **If something's missing, ask early.** Special pays sometimes need manual entry. For a line-by-line walkthrough, see [How to Read Your LES](/blog/how-to-read-your-les).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is hostile fire pay in 2026?

Effective October 1, 2026, Hostile Fire Pay is $450/month, up from $225. It's paid as the full monthly amount, not prorated, when you qualify. Before that date it was $225/month, a rate unchanged since October 2002.

### Did imminent danger pay go up?

Yes. Effective October 1, 2026, Imminent Danger Pay is $275/month, up from $225. It's prorated at $9.16 per day in a designated imminent danger area, up to the $275 monthly maximum.

### Why did hardship duty pay go down?

The memo that raised HFP and IDP also reduced the top Hardship Duty Pay–Location rate from $150 to $100/month. It gives the reason as consistency with the maximum allowed for combined payment of HDP-L with HFP, and of HDP-L with IDP. Members already drawing HFP or IDP at top-tier locations were limited to $100 of HDP-L before, so for them it's a wash. Members at top-tier locations who draw neither now receive $50/month less.

### Can you get both HFP and IDP?

No. You can't receive Hostile Fire Pay and Imminent Danger Pay for the same period of service. The memo directs the services and DFAS to make sure the two aren't paid at the same time. You can draw either one alongside Hardship Duty Pay–Location.

### When did the new rates take effect?

October 1, 2026. The memo was signed July 14, 2026. Periods of service before October 1 are paid at the prior rates.

### Does everyone on HDP-L get less now?

No. The $50 and $100 HDP-L rates didn't change. Only the top rate dropped from $150 to $100, and members drawing HFP or IDP at those locations were already limited to $100 before the change.
