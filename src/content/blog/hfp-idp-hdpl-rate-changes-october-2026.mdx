Effective October 1, 2026, three deployment-related pays changed. Hostile Fire Pay (HFP) rose to $450/month. Imminent Danger Pay (IDP) rose to $275/month, prorated at $9.16/day. And the top Hardship Duty Pay–Location (HDP-L) rate went *down*, from $150 to $100/month. Which of those changes reaches a given paycheck depends on which pays you draw — and for members who draw HDP-L together with HFP or IDP, on a combined-payment limit in the pay regulation that hasn't yet been updated for the new rates.

## Combat pay went up. Hardship duty pay went down.

Before October 1, HFP and IDP both had a maximum monthly rate of $225, unchanged since October 2002, according to the DoD release announcing the change. The new rates come from a memo signed July 14, 2026 by the Assistant Secretary of War for Manpower and Reserve Affairs. The same memo cut the top HDP-L rate.

All amounts in this post are gross monthly special pay, before any taxes or withholding.

## What changed

| Pay | Before Oct 1, 2026 | From Oct 1, 2026 | How it's paid |
|-----|-------------------|------------------|---------------|
| Hostile Fire Pay (HFP) | $225/month | $450/month | Full monthly amount, not prorated |
| Imminent Danger Pay (IDP) | Up to $225/month | Up to $275/month | Prorated at $9.16/day |
| Hardship Duty Pay–Location (HDP-L), top rate | $150/month | $100/month | Monthly, by location designation |
| HDP-L, other rates | $50 and $100/month | $50 and $100/month | Unchanged |

Pay for periods before October 1, 2026 was figured at the old rates. A September deployment month doesn't get the new amounts after the fact.

One structural difference is worth knowing. HFP is paid by the month: the pay regulation says HFP "will not be prorated," and a member receives the full monthly rate for the month in which the qualifying hostile fire event occurred. IDP is paid by the day: $9.16 for each day in a designated imminent danger area, up to $275 for the month. For the full picture of how these fit with the other deployment pays, see [Deployment Pay Explained](/blog/deployment-pay-explained).

## What this means for your monthly special pay

The change depends on which pays you draw. Here are the cases one at a time.

### Drawing HFP only

- Before: $225/month
- After: $450/month
- Change: **+$225/month** ($225 × 12 = $2,700 over a full year)

### Drawing IDP only

- Before: up to $225/month
- After: up to $275/month
- Change: **+$50/month** at the monthly maximum ($50 × 12 = $600 over a full year)

IDP is paid by the day, so the change in a partial month is smaller. See the proration section below.

### Drawing HDP-L at the top rate, with no HFP or IDP

- Before: $150/month
- After: $100/month
- Change: **−$50/month** ($50 × 12 = $600 less over a full year)

This is the one group whose special pay plainly goes down. The pay regulation's $100 HDP-L limit applies only when a member is receiving HFP or IDP, so without either, the full $150 was payable before October 1. Now the top rate is $100.

### Drawing HDP-L at the $50 or $100 rate, with no HFP or IDP

No change. Those rates didn't move.

### Drawing HDP-L together with HFP or IDP

This case has a documented answer for before October 1 and no published answer yet for after. The next section explains why.

## The combined HDP-L limit — documented before October 1, unsettled after

**Before October 1, the rule was explicit.** The DoD Financial Management Regulation (FMR), Volume 7A, Chapter 17, "Special Pay – Hardship Duty" (December 2024 revision), paragraph 3.1, says:

> When a member is in receipt of Hostile Fire Pay (HFP) or Imminent Danger Pay (IDP), then the maximum amount of HDP – L is $100. The total entitlement of HDP – L plus HFP or IDP in any 1 month may not exceed $325.

The same chapter repeats the limit in Table 17-2 (Rules 5 and 10: HDP-L is limited to $100 for a month in which HFP or IDP is payable) and in Table 17-1, Note 3. The chapter cites the authority for paragraph 3.1 as an Assistant Secretary of Defense (Manpower and Reserve Affairs) memo of September 26, 2017 — a policy memo, not a statute.

So before October 1, a member at a top-tier ($150) location who also drew HFP or IDP could receive at most $100 of HDP-L, and at most $325 combined in a month.

**After October 1, the published rule hasn't caught up.** The chapter predates the October 2026 change, and the numbers show it:

- Table 17-1, Note 3 still reads that the HDP-L monthly entitlement "decreases to a maximum payment of $100 when Service members are authorized a concurrent payment of $225 for HFP or IDP." That $225 rate no longer exists for either pay.
- The $325 combined ceiling equals the old $225 plus $100. The new rates don't fit under it: HFP at $450 is more than $325 on its own, and the new IDP rate plus the top HDP-L rate is $275 + $100 = $375.

The October 2026 memo says it reduced the HDP-L rate "consistent with the maximum amount allowed for combined payment" of HDP-L with HFP and with IDP, but it doesn't state what that maximum now is. Whether the $325 ceiling is being revised alongside the new rates isn't stated in any document we've reviewed, and we haven't tried to work it out here.

One more open point: the memo's stated reason is the combined-payment maximum, but the reduction it states — "from $150 to $100 per month" — isn't limited to members who also draw HFP or IDP. This post reads it as applying to the top rate generally, which is why the no-HFP/IDP case above shows −$50.

**What to do with this:** if you draw HDP-L together with HFP or IDP, compare what was actually paid on a pre-October LES with an LES from October or later, and ask your finance office how the combined limit is being applied to you under the new rates.

## The IDP proration detail

Partial months and whole months are two different questions, and only one of them is answered by the memo.

**Partial months — documented.** The memo says the $275 monthly IDP "is prorated at $9.16 per day based on the actual time spent in a designated imminent danger area."

| Days in an IDP area | At $9.16/day |
|---|---|
| 10 days | 10 × $9.16 = $91.60 |
| 15 days | 15 × $9.16 = $137.40 |
| 20 days | 20 × $9.16 = $183.20 |

**Whole months — not stated.** The memo documents the daily rate and the $275 monthly maximum, but it doesn't say how a whole month is paid. Note that 30 days × $9.16 = $274.80, not $275. If your LES shows one figure or the other for a full month in the area, don't conclude from the arithmetic alone that you were underpaid — the memo doesn't settle it, so check your LES and ask your finance office.

The FMR chapter on HFP and IDP (Volume 7A, Chapter 10, October 2024 revision, written for the old $225 rate) adds one related rule: daily proration "does not apply to the 31st of a month" for members on active duty for 30 days or more. A 31-day month isn't 31 days at the daily rate.

HFP has no equivalent. It's the full $450 for a qualifying month.

## What to check on your LES

- **Look for the HFP or IDP entitlement line** (and HDP if you're at a hardship location). After October 1, 2026, HFP should show $450 for a qualifying month, IDP up to $275, and top-tier HDP-L $100.
- **Expect only one of HFP or IDP for a given period.** The memo directs the services and DFAS to make sure members don't receive both "for the same period of service." FMR Chapter 10 applies this month by month: in its examples, a member who qualifies for HFP in a month receives HFP for that entire month, and IDP only for other periods. If both appear for the same period, it's worth asking your finance office early, before it turns into a debt.
- **Remember the cutover.** Days before October 1, 2026 are paid at the old rates, so a month that spans the change, or a late-posted correction for September, can legitimately show the old figures.
- **Check partial IDP months against the daily rate.** Days in the area × $9.16, up to $275. For a whole month, see the note above about $274.80 versus $275.
- **If you draw HDP-L with HFP or IDP,** compare your pre- and post-October HDP line, as described above.
- **If something's missing, ask early.** Special pays sometimes need manual entry. For a line-by-line walkthrough, see [How to Read Your LES](/blog/how-to-read-your-les).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is hostile fire pay in 2026?

Effective October 1, 2026, Hostile Fire Pay is $450/month, up from $225. It's paid as the full monthly amount, not prorated, for a month in which you qualify.

### Did imminent danger pay go up?

Yes. Effective October 1, 2026, Imminent Danger Pay is up to $275/month, up from $225. It's prorated at $9.16 per day in a designated imminent danger area, up to the $275 monthly maximum.

### Why did hardship duty pay go down?

The memo that raised HFP and IDP also reduced the top Hardship Duty Pay–Location rate from $150 to $100/month. It gives the reason as consistency with the maximum allowed for combined payment of HDP-L with HFP, and of HDP-L with IDP. Members at top-tier locations who draw neither HFP nor IDP now receive $50/month less. For members who draw both: before October 1, the pay regulation (FMR Volume 7A, Chapter 17, paragraph 3.1) limited HDP-L to $100 and the combined total to $325 a month. That chapter hasn't been updated for the new rates, so how the limit applies now isn't yet published — check your LES and your finance office.

### Can you get both HFP and IDP?

No. The memo directs the services and DFAS to make sure members don't receive HFP and IDP for the same period of service. FMR Volume 7A, Chapter 10 applies this month by month: in a month when HFP is paid, it covers that entire month, and IDP is paid only for other periods. Either one can be drawn alongside Hardship Duty Pay–Location.

### When did the new rates take effect?

October 1, 2026. The memo was signed July 14, 2026. Periods of service before October 1 are paid at the prior rates.

### Does everyone on HDP-L get less now?

No. The $50 and $100 HDP-L rates didn't change; only the top rate dropped from $150 to $100. Members at top-tier locations without HFP or IDP receive $50/month less. For members who also draw HFP or IDP: before October 1, the pay regulation (FMR Volume 7A, Chapter 17, paragraph 3.1) limited HDP-L to $100 and the combined total to $325 a month. That chapter hasn't been updated for the new rates, so how the limit applies now isn't yet published — check your LES and your finance office.
